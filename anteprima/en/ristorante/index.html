Restaurant & Pizzeria

Real Trentino cooking. Master-made pizza. No shortcuts.

The modern osteria of Val di Sole: ingredients from our valleys, traditional local recipes and a pizza worth the trip. Open to everyone, for lunch and dinner.

Modern osteria

If we wouldn't serve it to our own family, it doesn't reach your table.

A restaurant isn't judged by its furniture. It's judged by the plate. That's why we start with the produce: mountain ingredients that are seasonal and traceable, chosen from producers in our valleys. Then we treat them with respect, using the traditional recipes of Val di Sole and Trentino and today's technique.

01

Roots

The recipes we grew up with, from the traditions of Val di Sole and Trentino. Every dish starts there.

02

Season

The menu follows the mountain. We use what the valleys give at that moment, at its best.

03

Craft

Modern technique in service of flavour, not effect. Nothing superfluous on the plate.

The restaurant

Tradition, done today.

Trentino dishes reinterpreted with a modern touch, plus a few international options for those who want to leave the beaten path. Fresh ingredients, honest portions, clear flavours. The lunch you need between runs and the dinner you remember at the end of your holiday.

Pizza is a craft. Our master knows it.
