Experiences

Two seasons. A valley that never stops.

Dimaro Folgarida is the ideal starting point: slopes in winter, rivers and trails in summer. You choose the day, we take care of the evening.

Winter · December – April

Snow minutes away.

01

Folgarida-Marilleva

The lifts are minutes from the Locanda. From here you can ski all the way to Madonna di Campiglio in the Skiarea Campiglio Dolomiti di Brenta.

02

Tonale Pass

High-altitude slopes and the Presena glacier, for reliable snow all season long.

03

Cross-country and snowshoeing

Cross-country loops and snowshoe trails through the valley's woods, for those who prefer silence to speed.

Summer · June – October

Rivers, trails and two wheels.

01

Val di Sole cycle path

The cycle path runs through Dimaro and follows the Noce river along the valley. Perfect for families, e-bikes included.

02

Rafting on the Noce

The Noce is one of Europe's best-loved rivers for rafting and kayaking, with routes for beginners and experts.

03

Hiking in the parks

Stelvio National Park and Adamello Brenta Nature Park: trails, mountain huts and alpine lakes for every level.

The day is yours. The evening is ours.

Back from the slopes or the trail, go up to your apartment and come down for dinner. No car to take, no table to hunt for.
