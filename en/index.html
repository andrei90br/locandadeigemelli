The Locanda

One address. Two reasons to choose it.

Most mountain holidays make you choose: a good place to sleep or a good place to eat. Here you don't have to. Go up to your apartment, come down for dinner. Same people, same standard, from check-in to the last pizza.

Stay

Six apartments. Zero compromises.

Every apartment is independent, with a fully equipped kitchen, living area and private bathroom. Real space for couples, families and groups of friends, with the freedom of a home and a restaurant right downstairs.

Experiences

Val di Sole, right outside the door.
