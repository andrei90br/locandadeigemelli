A modern osteria

If we wouldn't serve it to our own family, it doesn't reach your table.

You don't judge a restaurant by its furniture. You judge it by the plate. That's why we start with the raw ingredients: seasonal, traceable mountain produce from the farmers of our valleys. Then we treat them with respect, using the recipes of the Val di Sole and Trentino tradition and the technique of today.
