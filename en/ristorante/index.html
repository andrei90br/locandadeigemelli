Restaurant & Pizzeria

Real Trentino cooking. Pizza by a master.

The modern osteria of Val di Sole: ingredients from our valleys, traditional local recipes and pizza by Barbara, our master pizzaiola. Open to everyone, for lunch and dinner.

Modern osteria

If we wouldn't serve it to our own family, it doesn't reach your table.

A restaurant isn't judged by its furniture. It's judged by the plate. That's why we start with the produce: mountain ingredients that are seasonal and traceable, chosen from producers in our valleys. Then we treat them with respect, using the traditional recipes of Val di Sole and Trentino and today's technique.

01

Roots

The recipes we grew up with, from the traditions of Val di Sole and Trentino. Every dish starts there.

02

Season

The menu follows the mountain. We use what the valleys give at that moment, at its best.

03

Craft

Modern technique in service of flavour, not effect. Nothing superfluous on the plate.

The restaurant

Tradition, done today.

Trentino dishes reinterpreted with a modern touch, plus a few international options for those who want to leave the beaten path. Fresh ingredients, honest portions, clear flavours. The lunch you need between runs and the dinner you remember at the end of your holiday.

Lunch 12:00 – 14:30 · Dinner 17:30 – 22:00

The pizzeria

Pizza is a craft. Barbara knows it.

Every pizza comes from the hands of Barbara, our master pizzaiola. Carefully made dough, quality seasonal toppings, considered pairings. You will find the classics you love and combinations created in our own kitchen.

Every evening 17:30 – 22:30, except Tuesdays

Beyond dinner

The same standard, wherever you want to eat.

Takeaway

Our cooking and our pizza to take away, for a dinner in your apartment or at home. Same care, same taste.

Summer garden

From June to October we serve outdoors in the garden: summer evenings, birthdays, dinners with friends and parties.

The taste of the valley

Cheese, cured meats, apples, flour and vegetables from Trentino, chosen from producers in our valleys. We know where every ingredient comes from. And you can taste it.

The valleys provide the ingredients. We bring the craft.
